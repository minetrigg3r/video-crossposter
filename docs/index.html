CrossPoster
Privacy Terms

Personal video publisher

One vertical video.
Your own accounts.

CrossPoster is a self-hosted app for posting one short video to your TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts accounts using each platform’s official APIs. It runs on your machine. It is not a public social network and it is not offered as a commercial product.

Official APIs only

OAuth login for TikTok, Meta/Instagram, and YouTube. No browser bots, no unofficial clients.

You keep the files

Videos and login tokens stay on the computer that runs the app. Tokens are encrypted at rest.

You stay in control

Pick which accounts get each video. Disconnect anytime. Revoke access in each platform’s settings.

This page exists so reviewers can read what CrossPoster is, plus the privacy policy and terms. There is no public signup.

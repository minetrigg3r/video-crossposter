Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

CrossPoster (“the app”) is a personal, self-hosted tool. The person who installs and runs it is the operator. This policy describes what the app accesses and stores when you connect accounts and publish videos.

You should read this policy before connecting any account. Connecting an account means you understand how the app uses that platform’s APIs.

YouTube API Services

CrossPoster uses YouTube API Services to connect YouTube channels and upload Shorts. Use of YouTube data through this app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

Google’s own data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

Depending on which platforms you connect, the app may access:

  • YouTube: your channel identity (name, channel ID, thumbnail) and permission to upload videos to that channel (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: basic profile info and permission to publish videos you explicitly submit.
  • Instagram: professional account identity linked to a Facebook Page, and permission to publish Reels you explicitly submit.

The app does not read your subscribers, comments, DMs, or watch history. It does not search YouTube or recommend videos.

What is stored, and where

Data is stored on the computer (or server) where you run CrossPoster, in a local SQLite database and local folders. It is not sent to a CrossPoster cloud. There is no CrossPoster company backend.

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens — stored encrypted (Fernet). These let the app post on your behalf until they expire or you disconnect.
  • Account labels — display name, username, avatar URL, platform user ID.
  • Video files you upload — stored locally for publishing, plus captions, titles, privacy choices, and post history (status, error text, and public video links if a platform returns them).
  • App logs — technical logs on disk for debugging failed uploads.

The operator of the install can see everything stored on that machine. If you run it only for yourself, that operator is you.

How the data is used

Stored data is used only to:

  • show connected accounts in the UI
  • upload the video and metadata you chose to the accounts you selected
  • refresh expired login tokens
  • show publish status and history

The app does not sell data, run ads, build advertising profiles, or share tokens with third parties other than the official TikTok, Meta, and Google/YouTube APIs needed to publish.

Cookies and device data

During OAuth, the local web UI may set a short-lived, httpOnly cookie so the login redirect can finish. That cookie is not used for tracking. The app does not place advertising cookies or analytics SDKs.

Revoking access and deletion

You can stop access in two places:

  1. In CrossPoster: Accounts → Disconnect. That deletes the stored tokens for that account from the local database.
  2. For Google/YouTube: revoke the app at Google Account permissions. After you revoke, CrossPoster can no longer call YouTube for that channel until you connect again.

You can also delete the local database file, upload folder, and log folder on the machine running the app to remove stored history.

Other platforms

TikTok and Instagram (Meta) have their own terms and privacy policies. Connecting those accounts is optional and uses their official OAuth flows.

Contact

This is a personal project. For questions about this policy or stored data, open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this site, or contact the person who installed the app on your machine.
